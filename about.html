How it works

Four steps to a fair split

QuickSplit tracks group expenses and works out who owes whom with the fewest transfers. No sign-up, and your data never leaves this device.

  1. Step 1

    Add members

    On the Members tab, add everyone involved in the trip, dinner or event. Short, recognisable names work best.

  2. Step 2

    Log expenses

    On the Expenses tab, add each cost:

    • Title — what it was, e.g. “Dinner at Luigi’s”.
    • Paid by — who paid and how much. Several people can pay for one expense.
    • Split between — who shares it. The amount is divided evenly between everyone selected.
  3. Step 3

    Check balances

    The Balances tab updates live. It shows what each person paid, their share of the bills, and whether they get money back or owe the group.

  4. Step 4

    Settle up

    Instead of everyone paying everyone back, Settle up lists the fewest transfers that clear every debt. Make those payments and you’re done. Both reports can be downloaded as PDFs.
