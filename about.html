⚡ How QuickSplit Works

A quick guide to splitting bills without ruining friendships.

QuickSplit makes it incredibly easy to track group expenses and calculate who owes who with the minimum number of transfers.

1️⃣ Add Members

Head to the Members tab and type in the names of everyone involved in the trip, dinner, or event. It's best to use short, recognizable names.

2️⃣ Add Costs

Go to the Costs tab to log your expenses. For each expense:

  • Title: What was the cost? (e.g., "Dinner at Luigi's")
  • Payers: Who paid for it, and how much? (Multiple people can pay for a single cost!)
  • Shared By: Select who is splitting this cost. QuickSplit automatically divides the amount evenly among everyone you select.

3️⃣ View the Summary

The Summary tab shows exactly how much each person paid overall, how much they consumed (their share of the bills), and their net balance (whether they need to pay the group or receive money back).

4️⃣ Simplify Transfers

This is where the magic happens. Instead of everyone paying everyone else back chaotically, the Simplify tab runs an algorithm to find the least amount of transactions needed to settle all debts. It outputs a simple "Who pays Who" list. Pay these, and you're fully settled!
